### 0.3 "72 EMA band": the **72-period EMA is a real old-bot concept**, an **"EMA band" is not**
`EMA(72)` was the original (March 2026) trend strategy's slow EMA on 1 h candles, later expressed as 18 × 4 h bars (= 72 h) and kept as the default of `bullish_ema_bounce(period=72)` in the production code; the live trend strategy actually ran EMA 3/9 on 4 h bars. No "EMA band/envelope/channel" exists anywhere in the corpus or the recovered code; the bands that do exist are Bollinger (20, 2σ), the ±1 % "touch zone" of the bounce helper, the March bot's alpha-**price** `VALUE_BAND` (0.0035–0.0050 τ Gaussian) and the value strategy's **depth** band (1500–3000 τ). "Distance from the EMA" did exist as a metric (DBot `trend_score = (close − EMA)/EMA`; `bars_above_below_ema`; `px_vs_moving = log(spot/moving_price)`). Treat the operator's phrase as a **new ask** to be defined (§3.4).
